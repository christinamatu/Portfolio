# 🐱 Feline Breeds and Characteristics Study Using Python
#### Overview
This study explores relationships between cat breeds and their physical and behavioral characteristics using Python for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization.

**Research Question**: Which cat breeds shed the least, and do those breeds also score well on other lifestyle traits?

#### Objectives
- Explore a dataset of cat breeds and their attributes
- Perform data cleaning and preparation
- Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Identify trends and relationships among breed characteristics
- Visualize key findings using charts and graphs

#### Dataset
The dataset contains information on multiple cat breeds, including:
- Breed name
- Temperament traits
- Intelligence rating
- Affection level
- Energy level
- Grooming requirements
- Weight range
- Life expectancy
- Other breed-specific characteristics

Source: [Cat Breeds Details](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/warcoder/cat-breeds-details)

#### Key Findings 
- Identified five cat breeds that best align with a low-maintenance lifestyle: Bambino, Donskoy, Peterbald, Sphynx, and Ukrainian Levkoy.
- All five breeds have a shedding level of 1, indicating very low shedding and minimal daily fur cleanup.
- All five breeds have a grooming need score of 2, requiring little brushing or coat maintenance.
- Each breed demonstrated strong family-friendliness, playfulness, and intelligence ratings, making them suitable companions for active households.
- The selected breeds have life expectancies ranging from approximately 8 to 14+ years, offering long-term companionship.
- Correlation analysis showed that low shedding and low grooming requirements are not associated with poorer overall health outcomes.
- Trait comparisons across shedding levels revealed that low-shedding breeds are just as playful and family-friendly as breeds with higher shedding levels.
- The analysis supports the conclusion that cat owners do not need to sacrifice health, personality, or sociability when choosing a low-shedding breed.
- Overall, the results provide evidence-based recommendations for individuals seeking low-maintenance cats that fit busy lifestyles while maintaining desirable behavioral traits.
